Purrrfolio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Purrrfolio is a Telegram collectible card game operated through the official Telegram bot at https://t.me/PurrrfolioBot. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and how users can contact us about their data.

Information we collect from Telegram

When you interact with the Purrrfolio bot, Telegram sends us information needed to run the game. This may include your Telegram user ID, chat ID, username, display name, language code, messages or button actions sent to the bot, group chat IDs where the bot is used, and payment events related to Telegram Stars purchases.

Game data we store

We store game progress such as your language preference, card collection, duplicate cards, pack ledger, crafting points, trades, marketplace listings, payment support requests, referral source, raffle participation, and anti-duplicate processing records for Telegram updates.

Information we collect from connected marketing platforms

If the official Purrrfolio team connects a social media or marketing account, we may use that platform's APIs to publish official Purrrfolio content, list content published by the official account, and read performance metrics such as views, likes, comments, shares, follower counts, and related account statistics. Connected platforms may include TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest, Threads, VK, RUTUBE, Bluesky, Mastodon, and similar services where Purrrfolio maintains an official presence. We do not use this access to post to third-party accounts without authorization.

How we use information

  • To operate the Telegram game and save player progress.
  • To deliver cards, packs, trades, referrals, raffles, and purchases.
  • To prevent duplicate processing, fraud, and abuse.
  • To provide player support and process eligible refunds.
  • To publish and measure official Purrrfolio marketing content.
  • To improve the game experience and understand organic campaign performance.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers needed to operate the game, including hosting, database, observability, Telegram, payment, and authorized social media platform APIs. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security of Purrrfolio and its users.

Data retention

We keep game data while it is needed to operate Purrrfolio, maintain purchase records, prevent duplicate rewards, resolve support requests, and protect against abuse. Marketing API tokens are stored only while the official Purrrfolio team needs the connected account for publishing or analytics.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time. Connected social platform access can be revoked in that platform's account settings. You can also contact us to request deletion of game data where deletion is technically and legally possible.

Children

Purrrfolio is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review the request.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Purrrfolio through the official Telegram bot: https://t.me/PurrrfolioBot.
